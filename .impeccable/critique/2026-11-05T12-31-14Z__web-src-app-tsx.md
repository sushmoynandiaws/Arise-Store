#### Overall Impression
The product details page possesses sound source-owned data integrity and robust modal dialog mechanics, but it suffers from purchase box clutter and below-the-fold panel sprawl. Converting the native select into tactile visual option chips, decluttering below-the-fold accordions, and streamlining the purchase actions into a dominant Royal Violet CTA will restore the quiet luxury of *The Artisan Pavilion*.

#### What's Working
1. **Source-Owned Integrity & Verifiable Calculation**: Real-time total calculation (`checkoutTotal`) updates seamlessly with quantity changes in tabular figures (`JetBrains Mono`), strictly reflecting AriseSell backend stock truths without simulated countdowns or fake stock scarcity.
2. **Accessible Native Dialog Framework**: Extended specifications, delivery policies, and seller terms open in a browser-native `<dialog>` (`product-info-dialog`) with URL hash synchronization (`#product-overview`, `#product-delivery`, `#product-terms`), ESC key cancellation, and background scroll locking.
3. **Bilingual Typographic Pairing**: Seamless harmonization of `Urbanist` headings with `Hind Siliguri` body copy, preserving proper line-height (`1.65`) and zero negative letter-spacing for Bengali script.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Layout Violation & Below-the-Fold Clutter (FAQs, Q&A, and Floating WhatsApp Widget)**
  - **Why it matters**: `AGENTS.md` explicitly mandates: *"Product pages follow a two-column image and purchase layout... do not add large information panels below the product."* Mounting `StoreFaqAccordion`, `ProductQuestions`, and a discordant `#25D366` green WhatsApp floating widget below the product pollutes the visual hierarchy and breaks the serene aesthetic of The Artisan Pavilion.
  - **Fix**: Remove the lower FAQ and Q&A panels from the main page; integrate FAQ content as an accessible tab within the existing `product-info-dialog` modal. Replace the floating green WhatsApp widget with a quiet, on-palette seller contact link inside the seller info box.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill` / `$impeccable layout`

- **[P1] Purchase Action Box Congestion & Responsive Breakdown**
  - **Why it matters**: Four controls (Quantity Stepper, Add to bag, Buy now, Save/Wishlist) are crammed into `.product-purchase-actions`. CSS breakpoint rules whipsaw erratically between `row` and `column` (`styles.css:360`, `769`, `802`, `975`), causing button crowding, label clipping, and decision paralysis.
  - **Fix**: Re-architect `.product-purchase-actions` into a clean two-tier layout:
    - Top row: Quantity Stepper + Dominant Royal Violet "Buy now" (`--primary`) pill.
    - Secondary row: Secondary "Add to bag" button and icon-only Wishlist button.
    - Standardize responsive behavior: clean column stack on `<640px` and structured two-tier row on `>640px`.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable shape` / `$impeccable harden`

- **[P2] Inaccessible & Primitive Variant Selection via Native `<select>`**
  - **Why it matters**: Variants are rendered in a native browser `<select>` dropdown (`App.tsx:2595`). This conceals option names, price deltas, and stock states until the OS picker is opened, and allows users to pick sold-out variants before learning they cannot be purchased.
  - **Fix**: Replace `<select>` with tactile option pills (`role="radiogroup"`). Display variant titles, price differentials (`+৳200`), and visually disable sold-out choices (`aria-disabled="true"`) with a clear diagonal strike and muted opacity.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt` / `$impeccable typeset`

- **[P2] The "Single Artwork" Barrier & Missing Size Chart Integration**
  - **Why it matters**: Physical craft goods (brass decor, nakshi kantha, leather goods) are restricted to a single non-interactive image container (`Art`), and `product.size_chart` is completely omitted from `ProductPage`. Buyers cannot inspect craftsmanship or verify physical proportions.
  - **Fix**: Add a multi-thumbnail preview rail below the main image when multiple media assets exist. Expose `product.size_chart` as an accessible trigger button in the specifications modal.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable bolder`

- **[P3] Redundant Pricing Display & Over-Aggressive Description Clamping**
  - **Why it matters**: When quantity is 1, the identical price is displayed twice within 40 pixels (`.detail-price` and `.product-purchase-total`). The brief description is clamped to just 2 lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2`), forcing unnecessary modal opens for short descriptions.
  - **Fix**: Hide the redundant "Total price" callout when `quantity === 1`. Relax description clamping to 4–5 lines on desktop before showing the "Read full description" link.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable quieter` / `$impeccable polish`
